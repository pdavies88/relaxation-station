# Guided meditation scripts for Relaxation Station.
#
# Format:
#   ## <meditation id>       starts a meditation (ids match content/catalog.json)
#   any other line           is spoken, then followed by a short flexible pause
#   ~                        adds a longer flexible pause
#   [N]                      adds an exact pause of N seconds (used for breath counts)
#   # comment                is ignored
#
# The generator spreads the flexible pauses so each session lasts exactly its listed length.
# Keep spoken words to about half of the session so there is room for silence.

## sleep-3
Welcome. Let's take a few quiet minutes to settle in for the night.
Lie down, or sit somewhere comfortable, and let your eyes gently close.
~
Take a slow breath in through your nose.
And let it go, slowly, through your mouth.
Once more. Breathe in.
And breathe out, letting your shoulders drop.
~
Let your breathing find its own easy rhythm. There's nothing you need to do.
Feel the weight of your body resting on the bed beneath you.
Let your jaw loosen. Let your hands grow soft and heavy.
~
If thoughts about today appear, that's okay. Notice them, and let them drift past like clouds.
The day is done. You can put it down now.
~
With every breath out, sink a little deeper into rest.
~
Stay here as long as you like. Let sleep come in its own time.
Goodnight.

## sleep-5
Welcome. This is your time to let go of the day and drift toward sleep.
Settle into a comfortable position, and let your eyes close.
~
Breathe in slowly, filling your lungs.
And breathe out, long and slow.
Again, breathe in.
And out, letting the bed hold all of your weight.
~
Now bring your attention to the top of your head.
Imagine a warm, gentle heaviness beginning there, and slowly flowing down.
It softens your forehead, and the small muscles around your eyes.
It relaxes your cheeks, your jaw, and your tongue.
~
The warmth flows down your neck and across your shoulders.
Let them sink, away from your ears.
It moves down your arms, all the way to your fingertips.
Your arms feel heavy, and completely at rest.
~
The warmth spreads through your chest and your belly, rising and falling with each breath.
It flows down through your hips, your legs, and into your feet.
Your whole body is heavy, warm and still.
~
There is nowhere to go and nothing to solve right now.
Just the gentle rhythm of your breathing.
~
If your mind wanders, simply return to the feeling of being held.
~
Let yourself drift. Let sleep arrive whenever it's ready.
Goodnight.

## sleep-10

~

Let that heaviness move up into your ankles, your calves, and your knees.
Your legs are completely relaxed. There's nothing they need to do.
~
Let the relaxation rise into your hips and lower back.
Feel your back spread out and sink down, fully supported.
~
Now your belly. Let it be soft. Let it rise and fall on its own.
Your chest relaxes too. Each breath out lets go a little more.
~
Bring your attention to your hands. Let your fingers uncurl.
Your hands are warm and heavy, resting easily.
The heaviness travels up your arms, into your shoulders.
Let your shoulders melt away from your neck.
~
Soften your neck. Let your head feel heavy on the pillow.
Relax your jaw, and let your teeth part slightly.
Smooth the space between your eyebrows.
Let your whole face be calm and still.
~
Your entire body is resting now, heavy, warm and safe.
~
Now imagine a quiet place where you feel completely at ease.

~
If a thought about tomorrow appears, imagine placing it in a small box and setting it aside. It will be there in the morning.

~
Let your breathing slow even further.
Each breath out carries you a little deeper into rest.
~
Deeper and deeper.
~

## stress-3
Welcome. Let's take a short break to reset.
Sit comfortably, with your feet on the floor. You can close your eyes, or rest your gaze on one spot.
~
Take a deep breath in.
And let it out slowly, like a long sigh.
Once more. In.
And out.
~
Notice how you're feeling right now, without trying to change it.
Maybe there's tightness in your shoulders, or a busy feeling in your mind. That's okay.
~
On your next breath out, let your shoulders drop.
Unclench your jaw. Soften your hands.
~
Now notice three things you can hear around you. Just listen.
~
This moment is all you need to handle right now. Not the whole day. Just this breath.
~
Take one more deep breath in.
And let it go.
When you're ready, open your eyes, and carry this calm with you.

## stress-5
Welcome. For the next few minutes, you can put everything else on hold.
Sit or lie down comfortably, and let your eyes close.
~
Take a slow breath in.
And breathe out, a little longer than you breathed in.
Again. In.
And a long, slow breath out.
~
Now gently notice what you're carrying today.
Maybe it's a worry, a long to-do list, or a difficult conversation.
You don't need to solve it right now. Just notice it's there.
~
Imagine holding that worry in your hands, like a stone.
Feel its weight.
Now imagine gently setting the stone down beside you.
It's still there. You can pick it up later if you need to. But for now, your hands are free.
~
Notice how your body feels without that weight.
Let your shoulders soften. Let your breath slow down.
~
If something else comes up, do the same. Notice it. Hold it. And set it down.
~
Breathing in, I know I'm breathing in.
Breathing out, I let go.
~
Breathing in, calm.
Breathing out, ease.
~
Stay with this quiet for a moment longer.
~
When you're ready, take a deep breath, wiggle your fingers and toes, and open your eyes.
Remember, you can set things down whenever you need to.

## stress-10

Find a comfortable position, sitting or lying down, and gently close your eyes.
~
Start with a few deep breaths.
Breathe in slowly through your nose.
And breathe out through your mouth.
Again. In.
And out.
One more time, in.
And all the way out.
~
Let your breathing return to normal.
Notice where you feel it most. Perhaps in your nose, your chest, or your belly.
Rest your attention there.
~
Now imagine a lake on a windy day. The surface is choppy. The water is cloudy with sand and mud.
This is what a busy mind can feel like. Thoughts swirling, one after another.
~
You don't have to force the water to be still. You can't.
But if you simply wait, the wind begins to ease.
~
With each breath, imagine the wind growing calmer.
The waves become ripples.
The ripples become gentle movements on the surface.
~
Slowly, the sand begins to settle. It drifts down, to the bottom of the lake.
The water becomes clearer and clearer.
~
When a thought appears, picture it as a small leaf landing on the water.
Watch it float. Watch it drift away.
You don't need to follow it.
~
Return to your breath, and to the stillness of the lake.
~
Now the surface is smooth, like glass.
It reflects the sky perfectly. The clouds, the trees, the soft light.
This stillness is always underneath, even on stormy days.
~
Rest here, in the calm.
~
If your mind wanders, that's completely normal. Simply notice, and gently come back to the still water.
~
Breathe in, and feel the calm.
Breathe out, and let it spread through your whole body.
~
Stay with this stillness for a little longer.
~
~
Slowly begin to bring your attention back to the room.
Notice the surface beneath you, and the sounds around you.
Take a deep breath in.
And let it go.
When you're ready, open your eyes. You can return to calm waters whenever you need them.
