# Guided meditation scripts for Relaxation Station.
#
# Format:
#   ## <meditation id> <bed>  starts a meditation. Ids match content/catalog.json. The bed is the
#                            background under the voice: warm, guitar, ocean, piano or flute.
#   any other line           is spoken, then followed by a short flexible pause
#   ~                        adds a longer flexible pause
#   [N]                      adds an exact pause of N seconds (used for breath counts)
#   # comment                is ignored
#
# The generator spreads the flexible pauses so each session lasts exactly its listed length.
# Keep spoken words to about half of the session so there is room for silence.
#
# Voice: warm and professional. Calm, grounded images; a light touch of personality, never silly.

## sleep-3 ocean
Hello, and welcome. You've reached the end of the day. Let's take a few quiet minutes to settle in.
Lie down and get comfortable, and let your eyes close whenever they're ready.
~
Let's start with a big, slow breath in through your nose.
And let it out through your mouth, slow and unhurried.
One more. Breathe in.
And out. Let your shoulders drop a little lower.
~
Now let your breath find its own rhythm. It knows the way. It has been doing this all day, without any effort from you.
Feel the bed holding you up. You don't have to hold anything up anymore.
Let your jaw loosen. Let your hands grow soft and heavy.
~
If thoughts about today come wandering in, that's alright. Notice them, and let them drift past like clouds.
The day is finished. You can set it down now.
~
With every breath out, sink a little deeper into the pillow.
~
There's nothing else you need to do. Sleep knows exactly where to find you.
Goodnight.
