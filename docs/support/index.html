Support

Need help? Email preston5@protonmail.com. I usually reply within a few days. The answers below cover the most common questions.

Is everything really free?

Yes. All 15 meditations and 12 soundscapes are free. There are no subscriptions, in-app purchases or ads.

Does it work without internet?

Yes. Everything is built into the app, so it works in airplane mode, on a plane, or anywhere without signal.

How do I fall asleep to a sound?

Open the Sounds tab and tap a sound. It plays on repeat until you stop it. You can lock your phone and the sound keeps playing. To stop it, use the pause button on your lock screen, or open the app and tap the stop button.

The sound stops when I lock my phone

  • Make sure your phone isn't in Low Power Mode with aggressive battery settings. On Android, check that battery optimization isn't restricting Relaxation Station (Settings → Apps → Relaxation Station → Battery → Unrestricted).
  • On Android 13 and later, allow notifications for the app. Android uses the playback notification to keep audio running in the background.
  • Another app that plays audio, such as a video or music app, can take over. Start the sound again after closing it.

I can't hear anything

  • Turn up your volume with the side buttons while a sound is playing.
  • On iPhone, the app plays even when the silent switch is on.
  • Check that your headphones or a Bluetooth speaker aren't connected to another device.

Can I use it for my child's naps?

Yes. Car Ride and Gentle Shush were made with little ones in mind, and Heartbeat and Brown Noise work well too. Keep the volume low, about as loud as a quiet shower, and place your phone a few feet away from the bed, not in the crib.

How long are the meditations?

Each of the five categories (Sleep, Stress Relief, Breathing, Body Scan and Focus) has a 3, 5 and 10 minute session. You can skip back or forward 15 seconds in the player.

Who made the meditations?

The meditation scripts were written for this app and narrated with an open-source Kokoro text-to-speech voice. The music box lullaby is Brahms' Lullaby, which is in the public domain. The guitar lullaby is an original tune. All music and sounds were synthesized for this app.
