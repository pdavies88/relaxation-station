Relaxation Station

Breathe, rest and drift off.

Relaxation Station is a free iPhone and Android app with guided meditations and soothing soundscapes. Everything is built into the app, so it works anywhere, even in airplane mode.

  • 15 guided meditations. Sleep, Stress Relief, Breathing, Body Scan and Focus, each in 3, 5 and 10 minute sessions.
  • 10 soundscapes. Gentle rain, wind chimes, ocean waves, a music box lullaby, a heartbeat, a crackling fire, a guitar lullaby, a fan, brown noise and soft bells. Each plays on repeat until you stop it.
  • Keeps playing with your screen locked. Fall asleep to a sound, like a sound machine.
  • Completely private. No accounts, no ads, no tracking, and no internet needed.
